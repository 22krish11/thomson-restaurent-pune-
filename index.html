KERALA. ON A PLATE.

Thomson Restaurant is a family table in New Sangavi. We cook Kerala the way it’s eaten back home — parotta pulled apart by hand, kothu chopped loud on the iron plate — alongside the North Indian, Mughlai and Chinese dishes a mixed crowd actually wants.
